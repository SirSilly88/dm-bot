Data Deletion: Tom Swoope DM Bot

Tom Swoope DM Bot does not keep a database of users. The only information it holds is temporary (comment and message IDs kept in memory for up to 7 days to prevent duplicate replies), and it is erased automatically.

To request deletion

Email signupwithtom@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and your Facebook or Instagram name. We will delete anything associated with you within 30 days and confirm by email.

You can also remove the conversation from your own inbox at any time in Messenger or Instagram, and you can delete any comment you left on our posts.
