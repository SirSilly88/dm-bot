Privacy Policy: Tom Swoope DM Bot

Tom Swoope DM Bot is an automated assistant for the Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts run by Tom Swoope (including First Off In My Opinion, Tom Swoope Events and Taking Care Of Family). When you comment a keyword on one of our posts or send us a keyword in a direct message, the bot replies with the link you asked for.

What we receive

Meta sends us the text of the comment or message, your public name or username, and the ID Meta assigns to you for our Page or account. We only use it to decide whether your message contains a keyword and to send you the matching reply.

What we store

Nothing is saved to a database. Comment and message IDs are held in memory for up to 7 days only to avoid sending you the same reply twice, and are erased whenever the service restarts. Server logs that may include the text of a keyword message are kept by our host for a short period and are not used for anything else.

What we share

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. Replies are sent through Meta's own messaging platform.

Your choices

You can stop interacting with the bot at any time. To ask for any data about you to be deleted, see our data deletion instructions or email signupwithtom@gmail.com.
